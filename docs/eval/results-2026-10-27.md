## One change: the judge prompt

Before: the strict-instructor prompt from spike 2. After: the same, plus "the expert's last image is the finished state; if the trainee's parts are in that state, say done even when the work itself is out of view; judge mainly by the latest image; say unclear only when the deciding part is hidden, blurred or out of frame". Same 90 cases, same frames, `CoachPolicy.passConfidence` 0.8 in both.

| Metric (90 cases)       | Before | After |
| ----------------------- | ------ | ----- |
| Pass rate (target 85%)  | 67%    | 76%   |
| Catch rate (target 90%) | 82%    | 79%   |
| False passes (target 0) | 10     | 12    |
| False unsafe stops      | 0      | 0     |

Threshold sweep after the change:

| Pass confidence | Pass rate | Catch rate | False passes |
| --------------- | --------- | ---------- | ------------ |
| 0.6             | 76%       | 77%        | 13           |
| 0.7             | 76%       | 77%        | 13           |
| 0.8             | 76%       | 79%        | 12           |
| 0.9             | 58%       | 88%        | 7            |
| 0.95            | 30%       | 93%        | 4            |

Before the change, 0.9 gave 52% / 88% / 7, so at 0.9 the new prompt passes more with the same catches and false passes. **Kept the new prompt; `passConfidence` stays 0.8 until the gold eval** (candidate: 0.9). The coach also needs two agreeing samples before it advances, which these single-sample numbers do not include, so live false passes should be lower.

## What the misses show

- Steps whose finish cannot be seen in a still (screws under the case, grease on a hidden face) were judged `not_yet` or `unclear`; the prompt change fixed some of these.
- Start-of-step frames of short steps often already look finished, so several "false passes" are label noise in the synthetic sets.
- Blur, hands in the way and close-ups produce `unclear`, which the coach turns into "show me closer", the intended behaviour.

## Cost

Muse Spark judge: about $0.0093 per check. A coached step needs about two checks, so a coaching minute costs about $0.02 to $0.03 (target $0.05). Guide builds: $0.58 for the 7.7-minute PS1 video; about $0.25 to $0.45 for the others.
