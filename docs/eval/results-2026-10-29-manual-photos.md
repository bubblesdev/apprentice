# Manual photos to a guide, first real run: 29 Oct 2026

**Source:** pages 8 and 9 of "Operation and Maintenance Manual for Hand Pumps, 2nd edition" (USDA Forest Service, 1999; public domain, via Wikimedia Commons). Page 8 has six numbered repair steps that refer to parts by key number; page 9 is the exploded parts diagram that names them.

**Call:** one Muse Spark request, two page images sent inline, through the request-hash cache.

## Result

| Check                                       | Result                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| All numbered steps found, in order          | 6 of 6                                                                  |
| Cover text, intro and parts table left out  | Yes                                                                     |
| The manual's CAUTION kept as a step warning | Yes: "Do not allow piston rod to turn"                                  |
| Parts named, not key numbers                | Yes (cap, jam nuts, piston washer, spacer ring, outer shroud assembly)  |
| Safety level                                | none on every step, which is right (no mains, blades, heat or pressure) |
| Time and cost                               | 13.3 s, $0.018                                                          |

Each step got a camera check the coach can use, such as "Handle disconnected and outer shroud lifted off with inner shroud and packing nut exposed".

## Weak spots

- Step 2's check adds a stacking order for the removed parts that the manual does not ask for. The coach could hold a trainee on a detail that does not matter.
- Step 4 is an inspection with a measurement (a 1/16-inch gap), which a camera can't confirm. The guide should mark it as one the trainee confirms by tapping, not one the coach judges.

Neither blocks the feature. Both are prompt changes for later.
